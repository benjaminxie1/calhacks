# Golden Wok Restaurant Group — CAM Statement Review SOP (rev. 2026-01)

Owner: Dana Ortiz (Lease Administration Lead). Approver of record: Sam Lee (Controller).
Accounts payable contact: Alex Kim.

## 1. The season
1. Each landlord sends one annual CAM reconciliation statement for the prior calendar year, usually February–May.
2. Accounts payable books every statement the day it arrives, as billed: the balance due to the landlord, net of the estimates we prepaid.
3. Lease administration then reviews the statement line by line against the lease before it is paid.
4. A printed total within $1 of the line-by-line recomputation is rounding. Accept the landlord's figure and note it; never dispute it.

## 2. Who is right
- **Pro-rata share, cap, management fee, exclusions, gross-up:** the lease (the abstract, checked against the lease text).
- **Real estate taxes:** the county assessor's bill for the parcel, when the lease says taxes are passed through as actually paid.
- **Estimates we paid:** our AP payment records, confirmed by the bank. The landlord's ledger is not proof.

## 3. Known findings and the fix
### 3.1 Share on occupied area instead of the center's GLA
The landlord divides our square feet by leased-and-occupied area, which raises our share when the center has vacancies.
Credit the difference line by line at the lease share and send the landlord a dispute notice citing the share clause.

### 3.2 Management fee over the lease limit
The fee is charged at a higher rate, or on taxes, insurance, capital items or the gross-up, which the lease leaves out of the base.
Credit the excess over the lease's maximum fee and send a dispute notice.

### 3.3 Controllable cap not applied
Controllable expenses rose more than the lease's annual cap over last year and the statement carries no cap adjustment.
Credit our share of the excess and send a dispute notice.

### 3.4 Capital item passed through
A roof replacement, parking lot resurfacing or structural repair is billed as a repair. The lease excludes capital costs.
Credit our share of the line and send a dispute notice.

### 3.5 Taxes above the county bill
Taxes billed exceed the county's bill for the parcel. Book taxes at the county figure and ask the landlord for the tax bills and proof of payment.
If the landlord does not answer within 2 business days, stop and give it to the Controller.

### 3.6 Estimate payment not credited
The statement credits fewer estimate payments than we made and the bank cleared.
Credit the missing payments and send the landlord the bank confirmation.

## 4. Controls
- No one approves their own entry. Credits over $2,500 and every new procedure need the Controller.
- Never edit a closed month. Months close on the 3rd business day; late credits post in the open month with a reference to the original statement.
- Every dispute must be raised inside the lease's audit window.
- If none of the above fits, stop and ask the Lease Administration Lead. Do not improvise.
