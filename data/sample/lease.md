# SHOPPING CENTER LEASE

**Landlord:** Mission Plaza Holdings LLC
**Tenant:** Golden Wok Kitchen, Inc., a California corporation
**Shopping Center:** Mission Plaza, 4100 Mission Boulevard, Hayward, California
**Assessor's Parcel Number:** 428-0061-012-03

## ARTICLE 1. BASIC LEASE PROVISIONS

1.1 **Premises.** Suite 106, containing approximately 2,400 rentable square feet.

1.2 **Shopping Center Area.** The Gross Leasable Area of the Shopping Center is 32,000 square feet.

1.3 **Commencement Date.** March 1, 2021. **Term.** Ten (10) years.

1.4 **Minimum Rent.** As set forth in Exhibit B.

## ARTICLE 5. OPERATING EXPENSES AND REAL ESTATE TAXES

5.1 **Tenant's Share.** "Tenant's Share" means the percentage obtained by dividing the rentable square feet of the Premises by the total Gross Leasable Area of the Shopping Center, whether or not leased or occupied, which percentage is agreed to be 7.50%.

5.2 **Operating Expenses Defined.** "Operating Expenses" means all reasonable costs actually incurred by Landlord to operate, maintain, repair and insure the Common Areas of the Shopping Center, including landscaping, parking lot sweeping and repair, security, janitorial service, Common Area utilities, and premiums for property and liability insurance.

5.3 **Exclusions.** Operating Expenses shall not include: (a) costs of a capital nature, including roof replacement, structural repairs and replacement of building systems, as determined under generally accepted accounting principles, except that the cost of a capital improvement made to reduce Operating Expenses may be included, amortized over its useful life, but only to the extent of the actual savings; (b) costs reimbursed by insurance or warranties; (c) leasing commissions, attorneys' fees and costs of disputes with other tenants; (d) depreciation; and (e) any management or administrative fee except as provided in Section 5.5.

5.4 **Controllable Expense Cap.** Notwithstanding anything to the contrary, Tenant's Share of Controllable Operating Expenses for any calendar year shall not exceed Tenant's Share of Controllable Operating Expenses for the prior calendar year by more than five percent (5%). "Controllable Operating Expenses" means all Operating Expenses other than insurance premiums, Real Estate Taxes and Common Area utilities.

5.5 **Administrative Fee.** Landlord may include an administrative fee not to exceed ten percent (10%) of Operating Expenses, excluding insurance premiums, Real Estate Taxes and any capital costs.

5.6 **Real Estate Taxes.** Tenant shall pay Tenant's Share of Real Estate Taxes actually levied against the Shopping Center parcel and paid by Landlord for the calendar year, net of any refund or abatement.

5.7 **Annual Reconciliation.** Within one hundred twenty (120) days after the end of each calendar year, Landlord shall deliver a statement of actual Operating Expenses and Real Estate Taxes for that year. Any overpayment by Tenant shall be credited against the next Rent due.

5.8 **Audit Right.** Tenant may, within ninety (90) days after receipt of the annual statement, dispute the statement in writing and inspect Landlord's books and records. If the audit discloses an overcharge of more than three percent (3%), Landlord shall pay the reasonable cost of the audit.
